IS117 / November 2026 / Research checkpoint

Never Say Never.
The thinking behind the shirt.

A resilient message, two audience hypotheses, and a clear presentation that lets bold shirt graphics do the talking.

Research, personas, and feedback are ongoing. This website is a classroom demonstration, not a functioning clothing store.

Audience / positioning

Two starting points.
One shared mindset.

Primary: The Resilient Achiever

People facing everyday challenges who might appreciate a small, visible reminder to keep moving forward.

Secondary: The Individual with a Purpose

People who want their clothing to express ambition, perseverance, and personal identity.

Both audiences connect through determination, not a particular level of success. These are hypotheses, not validated customer profiles.

Two persona hypotheses

The reminder.
The declaration.

A shirt can symbolize encouragement or identity, but cannot guarantee progress or solve a person's problems.

  1. 01 / Hope

    Facing one setback after another

    Someone navigating a difficult period might want reassurance that the current moment need not define their whole story.

  2. 02 / Growth

    The goal-driven overcomer

    Someone building confidence or discipline might want to express the mindset that motivates them to continue improving.

Peer challenge / documentation

Questions that changed
the research direction.

The student reports instructor approval to use an AI-assisted conversation for the peer-feedback activity; it was not a human classmate interview.

  1. 01 / Audience

    Can both audiences feel welcome?

    Keep perseverance as the shared foundation rather than designing exclusively for already-confident people.

  2. 02 / Identity

    How can variety remain recognizable?

    Explore recurring kanji and sharp graphics. Limited motto customization is a proposal, not an implemented feature.

  3. 03 / Trust

    What supports a credible product?

    Use verifiable details, clear photos, and genuine evidence. Do not invent product materials, interviews, or reviews.

  4. 04 / Design

    What if the graphic matters more than the motto?

    Investigate curated alternate messages and a possible no-motto option. These are not available storefront features.

Decisions / still open

Design honestly.
Keep checking.

Current direction: Bold, athletic-inspired shirt graphics within a clean, restrained, navigable website. The existing website remains a demo and takes no payments.

Still to investigate: The right limits on motto customization, a possible no-motto choice, accurate material descriptions, and expert review of Japanese text before commercial use.
